A neighbor who's already done the work

I'm a Kettering 1-A resident on Peach Orchard Road. In 2025, I helped start a local grassroots Team focused on voter turnout across Kettering. It was my first time canvassing door to door, running a neighborhood email list, and doing data-driven outreach.

Today, I'm on the Communications Committee for the Montgomery County Democratic Party. My focus is modernizing how the party communicates with all of us. My goal is to create systems that give you a voice and help the party listen to and understand the issues you care about most. We must also keep you informed about what's happening in our community, including what the party is doing on our behalf.

I'm building two experimental projects, and I'd love your help.

First, a new kind of newsletter that tells you directly what's going on, without the echo chamber or endless asks for money.

Second, a living survey that listens to what you care about, instead of telling you what you should care about. It gives you a way to speak up about the changes you want to see in our community, from our politicians, and in government.

I want to hear from you, and I'll make sure the party hears you too.

What is a Precinct Captain?

Your neighborhood's voice

A Precinct Captain is the Democratic Party's direct link to voters in a specific neighborhood — the boots-on-the-ground contact for our precinct.

Keep neighbors informed

Share factual information about candidates, local ballot issues, and how to register — so every neighbor has what they need at election time.

Get out the vote

Knock doors and make calls before elections — from school board races to presidential contests — to make sure registered voters actually show up.

Build community power

By connecting neighbors and relaying feedback to the county party, Precinct Captains strengthen local civic infrastructure from the ground up.
